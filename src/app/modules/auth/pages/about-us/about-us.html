About CFTrainer

Your companion for learning, training and growing on Codeforces.

What is CFTrainer?

CFTrainer is a learning companion built around Codeforces, designed to help students, competitive programmers and coaches track progress, organize training and stay motivated.

We don’t replace Codeforces — we build on top of it. CFTrainer connects your activity, statistics and training groups into one clear and friendly interface.

📊 Progress & Statistics

Visualize your rating evolution, solved problems, streaks and topic distribution to better understand how you are improving over time.

👥 Groups & Coaching

Train together. Coaches can create groups, assign problem sets and follow students’ progress, while students stay focused and accountable.

🎯 Structured Training

Organize assignments, deadlines and challenges so your practice becomes intentional instead of random problem solving.

What is Codeforces?

Codeforces is one of the most popular competitive programming platforms in the world. It hosts programming contests, practice problems and maintains a rating system that reflects a programmer’s skill level.

CFTrainer uses your Codeforces data to help you make sense of your journey and improve more efficiently.

Our Philosophy

Competitive programming is not just about solving problems — it’s about consistency, reflection and community.

CFTrainer was created to support learning, not pressure. To give structure without taking away curiosity. And to help you enjoy the process of getting better.

Built for students, coaches and competitive programmers who want to grow — one problem at a time.
